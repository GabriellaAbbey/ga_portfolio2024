A Mobile App Design for SAV

About the Project

Goal

The goal was to create a mobile app design. SAV, short for savvy, is an app that helps the user create outfits from their closet. Using AI to help organize and try on their pieces, SAV makes finding your fashion easier.

Research and Sketching

SAV: Find Your Fashion is just as trendy and bold as you are! It is your clothes, your closet, your style. I wanted create a simple design that would reflect current trends.
